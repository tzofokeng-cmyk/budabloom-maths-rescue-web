Chapter 9: Similar Triangles

Learn exactly when to MULTIPLY and when to DIVIDE.

🎯 Today's main idea

1. FIND the scale factor → DIVIDE

Take the side you are going TO and divide by the side you are coming FROM.

FROM 4 → TO 8

8 ÷ 4 = 2

2. USE the scale factor → FOLLOW THE DIRECTION

If the arrow goes to a bigger triangle, MULTIPLY.

If the arrow goes to a smaller triangle, DIVIDE.

🧠 The 2 rules — remember these

FIND = DIVIDE
Need to find the scale factor?
DIVIDE
USE = FOLLOW THE ARROW
Already have the factor?
Look at the direction.

Original / Small → New / Big = ×

New / Big → Original / Small = ÷

👀 See it — Same Shape, Different Size

BudaBloom rule: look first, calculate second.

Same shape → different size → corresponding sides match.
Small → Big = MULTIPLY
Big → Small = DIVIDE

What are similar triangles?

Similar triangles have the same shape. They can be different sizes.

Matching sides are called corresponding sides.

Small triangle: 4 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm
Big triangle: 8 cm, 10 cm, 12 cm

Every matching side grew by the same factor: 2.

🔎 FIND the factor — watch the arrow

FIND factor: TO ÷ FROM = 8 ÷ 4 = 2

Step 1 — FIND the scale factor

Small side = 4 cm. Big matching side = 8 cm.

Small 4 → Big 8

FIND: 8 ÷ 4 = 2

Scale factor = 2

Why divide? Because we are FINDING the factor.

Step 2 — USE the scale factor

Small → Big

4 → 8

Multiply

5 × 2 = 10

Big → Small

10 → 5

Divide

10 ÷ 2 = 5

🛑 STOP — Ask this question first

Am I FINDING the scale factor?
YES ↓
DIVIDE: TO ÷ FROM
NO ↓
FOLLOW THE ARROW
Small → Big = ×    |    Big → Small = ÷

This is the BudaBloom shortcut: FIND = DIVIDE. USE = FOLLOW.

✏️ Worked examples

Example A — Find the factor

Original = 5 cm, New = 15 cm.

15 ÷ 5 = 3

Scale factor = 3.

Example B — Use the factor, Small → Big

Small side = 7 cm, factor = 3.

7 × 3 = 21 cm.

Example C — Use the factor, Big → Small

Big side = 21 cm, factor = 3.

21 ÷ 3 = 7 cm.

🔍 Missing side: use the direction

Small triangle has a side of 6 cm. The scale factor is 4. Find the matching big side.

Small 6 → Big ?

Direction is Small → Big, so MULTIPLY.

6 × 4 = 24 cm.

Big triangle has a side of 24 cm. The scale factor is 4. Find the matching small side.

Big 24 → Small ?

Direction is Big → Small, so DIVIDE.

24 ÷ 4 = 6 cm.

⚠️ Common mistakes

  • Do not divide just because you see a scale factor. First ask: Am I finding the factor, or using it?
  • When FINDING the factor, divide TO ÷ FROM.
  • When USING the factor, follow the arrow.
  • Small → Big = multiply.
  • Big → Small = divide.
