⭐ Two simple proportion recipes

First decide: Is it DIRECT or INDIRECT?

DIRECT = same direction (UP→UP or DOWN→DOWN)
Recipe: DIVIDE first → MULTIPLY after.
Example: 3 pens cost R24 → R24 ÷ 3 = R8 → R8 × 6 = R48.
INDIRECT = opposite directions (UP→DOWN or DOWN→UP)
Recipe: MULTIPLY first → DIVIDE after.
Example: 4 people × 6 hours = 24 → 24 ÷ 8 people = 3 hours.

Remember: Direct = ÷ then ×. Indirect = × then ÷.

Topic 1: The Four Directions

UP → UP, DOWN → DOWN, UP → DOWN and DOWN → UP

Learn

Proportion compares two quantities that are related. Before calculating, look at how the quantities move.

UP → UP = same direction
DOWN → DOWN = same direction
UP → DOWN = opposite directions
DOWN → UP = opposite directions

Same direction is direct. Opposite directions are indirect.

Direct: ÷ first, then ×.
Indirect: × first, then ÷.
Worked example

3 pens cost R24. How much do 6 pens cost?

Pens: 3 → 6 = UP
Cost: R24 → more = UP

So it is UP → UP, which is direct proportion.

1 pen = R24 ÷ 3 = R8
6 pens = 6 × R8 = R48

Answer: R48.

Topic 2: Direct Proportion

Same direction = direct proportion

Learn

Direct proportion means both quantities move in the same direction.

UP → UP or DOWN → DOWN = Direct

When the price per item stays the same, you can find the value of one item first.

Worked example

4 kg costs R56. What will 7 kg cost?

1 kg = R56 ÷ 4 = R14
7 kg = 7 × R14 = R98

Check: 7 kg is more than 4 kg, so the cost should be more than R56. R98 makes sense.

Topic 3: Indirect Proportion

Opposite directions = indirect proportion

Learn

Indirect proportion means the quantities move in opposite directions.

UP → DOWN or DOWN → UP = Indirect

For the same fixed job, more workers usually means less time.

INDIRECT RECIPE
1. Multiply the known workers/people by the known time.
2. Divide by the new number of workers/people.
Example: 4 × 6 = 24, then 24 ÷ 8 = 3 hours.
Worked example

4 people take 6 hours. What if 8 people do the same job at the same rate?

People: 4 → 8 = UP
Time: 6 → less = DOWN

4 × 6 = 24
24 ÷ 8 = 3 hours

Answer: 3 hours.

Topic 4: Recognising Direct or Indirect

Name the relationship after looking at the directions

Learn

First look at the arrows. Then name the relationship.

UP → UP / DOWN → DOWN = Direct
UP → DOWN / DOWN → UP = Indirect
Worked example

More items at the same price per item means a higher cost.

Items = UP
Cost = UP

UP → UP = Direct proportion.

Topic 5: Choosing the Method

Direct: find one unit. Indirect: keep the product the same.

Learn

Choose your method after recognising the relationship.

Direct: find one unit, then multiply.
Indirect: multiply the known quantities, then divide by the new quantity.
DIRECT RECIPE: Divide first, multiply after.
INDIRECT RECIPE: Multiply first, divide after.
Worked example

3 workers take 20 days. If 12 workers do the same job, how many days?

Workers go UP, so time goes DOWN. It is indirect.

3 × 20 = 60
60 ÷ 12 = 5 days

Topic 6: Solve and Check

Choose the relationship, calculate, then check if your answer makes sense

Learn

Always do three things: choose the relationship → calculate → check.

If your answer does not move in the direction you expected, stop and check your working.
Worked example

6 shirts cost R900. What will 4 shirts cost?

6 → 4 = DOWN, so cost must go DOWN.

R900 ÷ 6 = R150 per shirt.
R150 × 4 = R600.

R600 is less than R900, so the answer makes sense.
