Topic 1 — What is an Equation?

An equation has an equals sign. Both sides have the same value.

x + 5 = 12

Our goal is to get x by itself.

Method 1 — ⭐ Golden Rule

WHAT WE DO ON THE LEFT, WE MUST ALSO DO ON THE RIGHT.

x + 5 − 5 = 12 − 5
x = 7

Method 2 — 🔄 Opposite Operation / Move

We use the opposite of +5, which is −5.

x = 12 − 5
x = 7

Choose the method that makes the most sense to you.

Check: 7 + 5 = 12 ✓

Topic 4 — Two-Step Equations

2x + 3 = 11
⭐ Method 1 — Golden Rule
2x + 3 − 3 = 11 − 3
2x = 8
2x ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2
x = 4

🔄 Method 2 — Opposite Operation / Move
Step 1: 2x = 11 − 3 = 8
Step 2: x = 8 ÷ 2
x = 4

Both methods give the same answer. Choose the method you understand best.

+ → −
− → +
× → ÷
÷ → ×
2x + 3 = 11
2x = 11 − 3
2x = 8
x = 8 ÷ 2
x = 4

Practice: 3x + 2 = 14

Topic 5 — Equations with Brackets

Method 1 — ⭐ Golden Rule / Divide First

2(x + 3) = 14

2(x + 3) ÷ 2 = 14 ÷ 2
x + 3 = 7
x + 3 − 3 = 7 − 3
x = 4

Method 2 — 🔄 Opposite Operation / Move

2(x + 3) = 14
2×x = 2x
2×3 = 6
2x + 6 = 14
2x = 14 − 6 = 8
x = 8 ÷ 2
x = 4

Check: 2(4 + 3) = 14 ✓

Shortcut

2(x+3)=14
2x+6=14

Topic 6 — Check Your Answer

Always put your answer into the original equation.

3x + 2 = 14
x = 4
3(4) + 2 = 14
12 + 2 = 14 ✓
